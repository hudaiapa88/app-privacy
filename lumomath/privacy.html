This Privacy Policy explains how LumoMath ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mathematics training application ("App"). LumoMath is built to work primarily on-device without a developer-controlled backend.

Offline-First Learning: LumoMath stores your practice sessions, progress metrics, streaks, review queue, and reminder settings on your device. The current build does not require a central account or a server that stores your learning history.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Learning Data Stored on Your Device

  • Onboarding preferences: Initial setup status, preferred language, and related local app choices.
  • Daily and weekly plans: Generated study plans, task lists, and active session state.
  • Practice history: Attempt records, completed sessions, session summaries, streaks, and checkpoint results.
  • Progress metrics: Mastery levels, review queue items, topic insights, recent performance, and reminder settings.

1.2 Reminder and App State Information

  • Reminder schedule: Whether reminders are enabled and the time you select.
  • Device-side app state: Session resume data and storage migration flags used to keep the app stable across updates.

1.3 What We Do Not Collect

LumoMath does not require you to create a central account to use the core learning experience, and the current build does not send your session history or mastery data to a developer-controlled backend.

2. Permissions Explained

Permission Purpose Data Handling
Notifications Daily study reminders Reminder schedule is stored on-device and used for local notifications
Exact Alarm Deliver reminders at the selected time No study history is sent to our servers

3. How We Use Information

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Local storage: LumoMath stores learning and progress data using on-device storage mechanisms.
  • No central backend: We do not operate a developer-controlled service that stores your daily plan, attempts, mastery records, or session history.
  • No mandatory account: You can use the app without creating an online profile for the core learning flow.
  • User control: Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes the locally stored records from your device.

5. Third-Party Services

Service Purpose Data Shared
Android or iOS local notification framework Show daily reminders on your device Handled locally by the operating system

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your learning history. In the current build, your study data is not shared with a developer-controlled backend.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Disable reminders: You can turn off notifications or exact alarms in your device settings.
  • Delete local data: You can clear app data or uninstall the app at any time.
  • No account deletion flow needed: The core learning experience does not depend on a central user account.
  • Review app permissions: You can manage notification-related permissions from your device settings.
