This Privacy Policy describes how Lingolate ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our language learning application ("App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy while providing you with powerful language learning tools.

Offline-First Design: Lingolate is designed to work primarily offline. Most features including translation, flashcards, and learning activities work without an internet connection. Your learning data stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 User-Generated Content (Local Storage)

The following data is stored locally on your device:

  • Flashcards: Custom flashcards you create for vocabulary learning
  • Learning Progress: Your study history, scores, and statistics
  • Preferences: App settings, selected languages, and themes
  • Translation History: Your recent translations for quick access

This data is stored using local databases (SQLite, Hive) and remains on your device unless you explicitly choose to sync it.

1.2 Optional Cloud Sync (Google Sign-In)

If you choose to enable cloud sync:

  • Google Account Info: Email address and display name for authentication
  • Synced Data: Flashcards and learning progress you choose to backup
Cloud sync is optional. You can use Lingolate without signing in. If you sign in, you can delete your cloud data at any time.

1.3 Third-Party Data Collection

Google AdMob: Our app displays advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings.

2. Permissions Explained

Permission Purpose Data Handling
Camera Scan text from images for translation (OCR) Processed locally, not stored or transmitted
Microphone Speech-to-text for voice input and pronunciation practice Processed on-device, not stored
Storage Save flashcards, dictionaries, and learning data Local storage only
Internet Cloud sync, ads, and online dictionary lookup Only when connected
Notifications Learning reminders and study streak alerts Local notifications only

3. Machine Learning Features

Lingolate uses Google ML Kit for on-device machine learning:

  • Text Recognition (OCR): Extracts text from camera images for translation
  • Translation: Translates text between supported languages
On-Device Processing: All ML Kit features run entirely on your device. Your photos, text, and translations are never sent to Google's servers through ML Kit.

4. Third-Party Services

Service Purpose Data Shared
Google ML Kit OCR and translation None (on-device processing)
Google Sign-In Optional account authentication Email, name (if signed in)
Google AdMob Advertising Device identifiers, usage data
Firebase (optional) Cloud sync backup Your synced flashcards and progress

5. Data Security

  • Local Encryption: Sensitive data is stored using secure storage mechanisms
  • No Server Storage by Default: Your learning data stays on your device
  • Secure Authentication: Google Sign-In uses industry-standard OAuth 2.0
  • Encrypted Transmission: All network communication uses HTTPS

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data may be shared in these cases:

  • Advertising data with Google AdMob
  • Your synced data with Firebase (only if you enable cloud sync)
  • Crash reports for app improvement (anonymized)

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Delete Local Data: Clear app data from your device settings
  • Delete Cloud Data: Sign in and delete your synced data from the app
  • Deny Permissions: You can deny any permission (some features may not work)
  • Opt-out of Personalized Ads: Adjust in your device's Google settings
  • Export Data: Export your flashcards and learning data from the app

8. Data Retention

  • Local Data: Retained until you delete the app or clear app data
  • Cloud Data: Retained until you delete it or request account deletion
  • Ad Data: Managed by Google AdMob per their privacy policy

9. Children's Privacy

Lingolate is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us.

10. International Users

If you use cloud sync, your data may be stored on servers located outside your country. By using cloud sync, you consent to this transfer.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
